## 📋 Overview
A comprehensive Power BI Operational Excellence dashboard combining 
Lean Six Sigma methodology with Sales, Operations, Finance, HR, 
and Supply Chain analytics across 6 business lines.

Built on an Enterprise Data Model with 14 tables, 29 relationships, 
and 516 DAX measures — designed to identify process improvement 
opportunities and support data-driven decision making.

## 🎯 Key Findings
| KPI | Value | Target | Status |
|-----|-------|--------|--------|
| OEE % | 82% | 85% | ⚠️ Below Target |
| Sigma Level | 4 | 6 | ⚠️ Improvement Needed |
| On-Time Delivery | 47% | 85% | 🔴 Critical Gap |
| First Pass Yield | 94% | 95% | ⚠️ Near Target |
| Forecast Accuracy | 97% | 95% | ✅ Exceeds Target |
| Budget Variance | +11% | <5% | 🔴 Overspend |

## 🔍 Green Belt Insights
1. **On-Time Delivery (47%)** — Systemic process failure, 
   not seasonal variation. Root cause: Supplier performance 
   (SPI avg 41/100). Recommendation: Supplier development program.

2. **268 At Risk customers vs 272 Champions** — Customer base 
   at critical tipping point. Recommendation: Immediate retention 
   campaign targeting At Risk segment.

3. **Process Cycle Efficiency 4%** — 96% of cycle time is waste. 
   Value-Added Time: 2h vs Total Cycle Time: 48h. 
   Recommendation: VSM analysis and waste elimination.

4. **Budget overspend 11% despite 97% forecast accuracy** — 
   Problem is cost control in execution, not planning. 
   Recommendation: Monthly budget review gates.
